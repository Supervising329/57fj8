About Green Horizon

Local landscaping built on dependable service.

We help South Shore homeowners and businesses create attractive outdoor spaces that are easier to enjoy and maintain.

Our story

More than a decade of careful outdoor work.

Green Horizon Landscaping started with neighborhood lawn care and a simple promise: arrive when expected, communicate clearly, and leave every property looking better.

Today, we provide lawn maintenance, seasonal cleanups, planting, landscape design, and hardscaping for residential and commercial properties across the South Shore.

Our mission

Healthy, functional landscapes made simple.

Our mission is to deliver reliable landscaping with thoughtful planning, clean job sites, and results that hold up season after season.
